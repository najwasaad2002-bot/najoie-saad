A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Good copy starts with understanding people.

I'm Najoie Saad, an MBA General Management student with an interest in creative copywriting, digital content, and brand communication.

I enjoy exploring what makes a brand distinctive: its voice, its audience, and the small details that turn a message into something memorable. My approach combines curiosity, structure, and creativity.

This portfolio brings together self-initiated concept projects created to demonstrate my writing and content thinking. The featured brand concepts are independent exercises and are not commissioned or endorsed by the brands.
