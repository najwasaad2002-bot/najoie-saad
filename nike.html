NIKE

SPORT & LIFESTYLE · AD CONCEPT

Start where you are.

A conceptual campaign encouraging people to return to movement without waiting for the perfect moment, body, or routine.

Independent concept projectCopywriting & contentPortfolio sample

THE BRIEF

One idea.
A clear purpose.

Reduce the pressure around getting started and make movement feel accessible through an encouraging, action-oriented message.

Target audience
Young adults and everyday movers who want to become more active but may feel intimidated by performance-focused messaging.

Tone of voice
Direct, energetic, human, and encouraging.

THE WRITING

Copy samples

Below are original writing samples developed for this independent portfolio concept.

Campaign line

You don't need a perfect start. Just a first step.

Short-form ad

No perfect plan. No perfect pace. No perfect day. Just you, showing up. Start where you are.

Social caption

Ten minutes counts. A walk counts. Trying again counts. Your progress doesn't have to look like anyone else's.

Headline alternatives

Your pace is a place to start. / Progress begins before you're ready. / Make the first move yours.

THE THINKING

Why this direction?

The concept leads with a human insight, then uses short sentences and repetition to create momentum. The message is motivational without shaming the audience.

This is an independent speculative concept created for portfolio purposes. It is not an official campaign and does not imply a working relationship with or endorsement by NIKE.
