CONCIERGE · BADARO

LOCAL HOSPITALITY · SOCIAL CAMPAIGN

Your usual spot. With a little extra.

A conceptual neighbourhood coffee-shop campaign centred on familiar faces, small rituals, and the feeling of having a local place to return to.

Independent concept projectCopywriting & contentPortfolio sample

THE BRIEF

One idea.
A clear purpose.

Create a distinctive local voice that encourages repeat visits and positions the café as part of the rhythm of the neighbourhood.

Target audience
Badaro residents, nearby workers, friends meeting between plans, and people who enjoy their neighbourhood café ritual.

Tone of voice
Friendly, familiar, relaxed, and rooted in everyday neighbourhood life.

THE WRITING

Copy samples

Below are original writing samples developed for this independent portfolio concept.

Campaign line

Your coffee is ready. Your corner is waiting.

Social caption

A quick coffee before the day takes off, a catch-up that turns into an hour, or your usual order just the way you like it. See you in Badaro.

Story sequence

Slide 1: Your usual order? / Slide 2: Your favourite corner? / Slide 3: We’ll leave the light on. See you soon.

Promotional copy

Make a little room in your day for a good coffee and a familiar face. Drop by Concierge in Badaro.

THE THINKING

Why this direction?

Local detail makes the copy feel specific and human. The message sells belonging and routine, not just a drink.

This is an independent speculative concept created for portfolio purposes. It is not an official campaign and does not imply a working relationship with or endorsement by CONCIERGE · BADARO.
