MANGO

FASHION · SEASONAL CAMPAIGN

New season. New perspective.

A conceptual seasonal campaign built around versatile pieces, personal expression, and getting more from a wardrobe.

Independent concept projectCopywriting & contentPortfolio sample

THE BRIEF

One idea.
A clear purpose.

Introduce a new-season edit through everyday versatility instead of relying only on urgency or discounts.

Target audience
People looking for modern, adaptable wardrobe pieces that work across different parts of their lives.

Tone of voice
Polished, contemporary, optimistic, and practical.

THE WRITING

Copy samples

Below are original writing samples developed for this independent portfolio concept.

Campaign line

Pieces that move with you.

Promotional copy

From the first plan to the last-minute change, find pieces that keep up with the way your days unfold.

Email subject line

Meet the pieces you'll reach for again

Product description

A clean-lined shirt with an easy fit and a softly structured shape. Wear it loose with denim, tuck it into tailored trousers, or layer it under a lightweight jacket.

THE THINKING

Why this direction?

The concept connects product versatility to real routines. It stays aspirational while giving the reader practical styling ideas.

This is an independent speculative concept created for portfolio purposes. It is not an official campaign and does not imply a working relationship with or endorsement by MANGO.
