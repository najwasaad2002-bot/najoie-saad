AIRBNB

TRAVEL · WEBSITE & STORYTELLING

Feel a little less like a visitor.

A conceptual travel campaign about the small local discoveries that make a new place feel personal.

Independent concept projectCopywriting & contentPortfolio sample

THE BRIEF

One idea.
A clear purpose.

Shift the focus from ticking off attractions to experiencing the everyday details that help travellers connect with a destination.

Target audience
Curious travellers who value local atmosphere, flexibility, and experiences that feel personal.

Tone of voice
Warm, sensory, curious, and quietly inviting.

THE WRITING

Copy samples

Below are original writing samples developed for this independent portfolio concept.

Campaign line

Arrive somewhere new. Find your own way in.

Landing page headline

Feel a little less like a visitor.

Landing page copy

Find the corner café you return to twice. Take the street that wasn't on your list. Learn where the neighbourhood slows down in the evening. Travel is made of the little things that become your story.

Social caption

The best part of a place might not be on the map. Leave room for the café, the conversation, and the unexpected turn.

THE THINKING

Why this direction?

Sensory details make the writing feel lived-in. The copy sells a feeling of discovery rather than listing practical features.

This is an independent speculative concept created for portfolio purposes. It is not an official campaign and does not imply a working relationship with or endorsement by AIRBNB.
