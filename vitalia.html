VITALIA

PET RETAIL · CONTENT CONCEPT

Little things. Lots of love.

A conceptual content direction for a pet shop, using warm and useful messaging for people who want to care for their companions every day.

Independent concept projectCopywriting & contentPortfolio sample

THE BRIEF

One idea.
A clear purpose.

Build an approachable voice that combines affection with practical guidance and helps pet owners discover everyday essentials.

Target audience
Pet owners who see their animals as family and appreciate friendly, clear product communication.

Tone of voice
Warm, helpful, reassuring, and playful without being overly cute.

THE WRITING

Copy samples

Below are original writing samples developed for this independent portfolio concept.

Campaign line

Everyday care for their big personality.

Social caption

The excited greeter. The professional napper. The curious explorer. Every pet has their own little ways. Find everyday essentials for the personality you love.

Product description

Make walk time easier with a comfortable, adjustable harness designed for everyday outings. Check the fit before each walk and choose a size suited to your pet.

Content idea

A weekly 'Pet parent tip' series with simple, responsible reminders about grooming, enrichment, and choosing suitable supplies.

THE THINKING

Why this direction?

The copy builds a friendly relationship with pet owners while keeping product descriptions clear and avoiding unsupported health claims.

This is an independent speculative concept created for portfolio purposes. It is not an official campaign and does not imply a working relationship with or endorsement by VITALIA.
