SEPHORA

BEAUTY · SOCIAL & PRODUCT COPY

Your routine. Your rules.

A conceptual beauty content direction that celebrates experimentation and helps customers choose products in a way that feels personal.

Independent concept projectCopywriting & contentPortfolio sample

THE BRIEF

One idea.
A clear purpose.

Make beauty feel welcoming and expressive rather than prescriptive, while encouraging exploration of products and routines.

Target audience
Beauty fans with different levels of experience who want to discover products that fit their style, time, and preferences.

Tone of voice
Playful, inclusive, confident, and helpful.

THE WRITING

Copy samples

Below are original writing samples developed for this independent portfolio concept.

Campaign line

Your routine. Your rules.

Social caption

A full glam kind of day? A five-minute routine? Both belong here. Find the textures, shades, and little favourites that make beauty feel like you.

Product description

A lightweight, buildable tint that gives skin a fresh-looking finish. Apply a little for a subtle wash of colour or layer it for a more defined look.

Email subject line

Your next beauty favourite might be one click away

Call to action

Find your new favourite

THE THINKING

Why this direction?

The copy balances personality with usefulness. It invites discovery without suggesting that one routine or look is right for everyone.

This is an independent speculative concept created for portfolio purposes. It is not an official campaign and does not imply a working relationship with or endorsement by SEPHORA.
